## Visibility plan

1. **Localise the listing** (Play Console → Translations): Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Indonesian, French, German, Arabic, Turkish, Russian, Vietnamese. Localised listings usually bring the biggest install increase for utility apps.
2. **Store listing experiments**: test the icon first (highest impact), then the first screenshot, then the short description. Run each test for at least 7 days.
3. **Ratings**: ask happy users to rate the app. An in-app review prompt after a user's 3rd saved PDF is the standard approach (I can add one with `expo-store-review`). Reply to every review within 48 hours.
4. **Quality signals**: keep crash rate under 1.09% and ANR rate under 0.47% (Android vitals). Above these, Google lowers your ranking.
5. **Release updates every 2–4 weeks**: frequent updates help ranking. Mention new features in "What's new".
6. **Early installs**: share the link in student, office and small-business groups. Early installs plus good ratings in the first 30 days strongly affect ranking.
7. **Paid installs (optional)**: Google App Campaigns with target CPI around $0.05–0.15 in Tier-3 countries (India, Indonesia, Brazil, Nigeria) for volume. Only scale up if ad revenue per user is higher than cost per install.

## Revenue setup checklist

- [ ] AdMob → Privacy & messaging: publish the **GDPR message** (EU traffic earns nothing without consent)
- [ ] Publish **app-ads.txt** on your developer website (unverified apps lose a lot of demand)
- [ ] Link AdMob to the Play app once it is live
- [ ] AdMob → Mediation: add Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity and Liftoff with bidding. More ad networks competing for each impression typically raises revenue per thousand impressions noticeably
- [ ] Turn on **eCPM floors** (optimized) for interstitial and rewarded units
- [ ] Watch these numbers weekly: impressions per daily user, eCPM by country, day-1/day-7 retention. If day-1 retention drops below about 25%, reduce interstitial frequency. Retention is worth more than extra impressions
